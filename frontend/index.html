Operating System Concepts Modeled
  • Process States & Lifecycle: Apps transition between FOREGROUND, VISIBLE, SERVICE, BACKGROUND, CACHED, and STOPPED.
  • Scheduling & CPU Accounting: Priority-weighted round-robin scheduler allocates CPU slices per tick, tracking cpu_time_ms and context switches.
  • DVFS (Dynamic Voltage & Frequency Scaling): CPU scales across 600 / 1200 / 1800 / 2400 MHz based on aggregate runnable demand.
  • Doze Mode & App Standby Buckets: Screen-off idle triggers Doze; RARE and RESTRICTED buckets throttle background CPU/network.
  • Wakelocks: Background services holding wakelocks prevent full sleep and incur explicit mA overhead.
  • Power Attribution Model: Hardware component mA (power_profile.xml) is attributed proportionally to runnable processes each tick.
  • Sampling vs Event-Based Logging: Battery & energy samples are logged periodically to SQLite; process state transitions are logged as discrete events.
